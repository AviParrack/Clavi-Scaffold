## Guiding Principles

- **Gardening for the Brain**: Cultivate mental patterns intentionally — planting beneficial habits, rooting out unhelpful ones. Iterative. The goal is a mind you enjoy inhabiting.
- **Idealism and Pragmatism**: Not opposing. Ideals = compass, pragmatism = razor for "how to move closer from where I am."
- **Myopia and Haze**: Limited perspective, imperfect memory, reality obscured by uncertainty. Epistemic humility. Seek goods that work across varied worldviews.
- **You Are A Machine**: Billions of neurons, trillions of synapses. Understanding yourself = lifelong engineering challenge. Patience. Scientific thinking.
- **We're Also Just Monkeys**: Evolutionary baggage. Balance seriousness with absurdity. Rapidly switch between modes.
- **Learn Voraciously**: Knowledge uniquely compounds — you keep it while giving it away.

## Bifurcation of the Mind

**The Planner** — monthly to yearly timescale:
- Vision, values, big goals, personal philosophy, boundaries, strategy
- Deep reflection on experiences, holistic perspective

**The Driver** — second to hourly timescale:
- Execution, task decomposition, presence, immediate reaction, improvisation
- Monitors internal and external conditions with high fidelity

**Neither should dominate.** Over-thinking (planner dominance) paralyzes. Under-thinking (driver dominance) sacrifices long-term goals. Excellence = switching seats fluidly, recognizing each mode's failure pattern.

## Systems for the Planner

### Habit Accrual/Deletion
Two-month cycles. One habit to build, one to break per cycle. Six improvements and six eliminations annually. Develops meta-awareness about behavioral pattern recognition.

### System Testing
N=1 research. Form hypotheses about mechanisms creating desired/undesired experiences. Run personal experiments. Caution about causation vs correlation.

### Fuel Sources
Energy scarcity isn't the real problem — motivation scarcity is. Three sources:

1. **Motivation** — premium fuel from excitement or fear. Collect motivators. Fix broken reward loops with intermittent reinforcement.
2. **Inertia** — motion by default. Reduce friction for good, increase for bad. Automate positive defaults.
3. **Willpower** — backup power. Don't rely on it permanently. Use it to bring motivation into alignment with values. The goal: becoming someone naturally motivated toward valued activities.

### Planning
Long-term direction deserves dedicated attention. Group workshops (Hot Seat Protocol) for constructive feedback on goals.

## Systems for the Driver

### Emotion Processing Algorithm
Emotions compress massive simultaneous data into actionable feeling. Real but not necessarily reliable.

1. **Pause** and assess what information this emotion compresses
2. **Check urgency** — does this require immediate action?
3. **Decide response**: take action / acknowledge and wait / investigate further

*Example: Sunday evening anxiety = work stress + weekend regret + fatigue + overstimulation. Appropriate: plan tomorrow, tea, reasonable bedtime.*

### Growth Windows
Highest sustainable pace — pushing beyond comfort without lasting damage. "By day's end, you want to happily collapse exhausted, tired right to the edge of recovery by morning, but not beyond."

Occasionally (safely) push past breaking point deliberately. Teaches true capability and builds recovery confidence.

### Force It 'til You Feel It
New activities don't feel natural initially — brain hasn't integrated them. Deliberately adopt worthy activities until they feel internally real. Better framing than "fake it till you make it."

### Brain/Body
When mental discomfort is excessive, retreat into body-focused activities demanding present attention — exercise, meditation. Mental respite.

## Miscellaneous Hacks

- **Association Clusters**: Deliberately construct memory clusters through repeated patterns (ritual + music + context → potent emotional trigger)
- **Cached Rewards**: Withhold something loved until a milestone
- **Shortcutting Subvocalization**: Speed up thought by skipping full internal articulation
- **Last Day** 🚩: Countdown of estimated remaining days. Motivating but risks distress.
- **Sweet Synergies**: Combine activities — walk + lunch + audiobook
- **Gaslight Yourself** 🚩: "Maybe just 10 minutes" → momentum → continuation
- **Thought Games**: Historical figures as imaginary conversation partners
