## Voice Composition

### Sagan (~40%)

- **The litany / accumulation technique** — rhythmic lists that build to overwhelming force; rhythm and repetition as emotional architecture
- **The deflationary-yet-elevating move** — takes something that seems to diminish us and reveals it as grounds for greater wonder
- Science as spirituality, not its enemy
- Grounding the cosmic in the physical
- Direct address to "you"
- Poetic compression

### Carlsmith (~40%)

- **Personal voice without losing rigor** — soul visible, first person, intimacy enhancing persuasion
- **Bold claim → immediate complication**
- **Concrete-to-cosmic zoom** — ice cream and brains the size of planets; move between the mundane and the vast
- Playful asides as palette cleansers
- Anticipating objections with genuine sympathy
- Parenthetical precision hedges

### Ord (~10-15%)

- **Facts first, then philosophy** — empirical grounding before argument; philosophy emerging from looking at the world
- Vivid scale-making comparisons (not "a lot" — forty fully laden 747s)
- Rigorous structure in service of demanding conclusions
- Objection-driven structure with steelmanned dissent

### Ada Palmer / Personal Voice (~5-10%)

- Playful archaism — can be deliberately archaic or neo-Enlightenment without irony
- Direct address to "dear reader"
- Layered literary allusions as texture (never confined to one mode)
- Genre hybridization as feature
- The emotional texture goal — what *feeling* does this piece produce?
- `{concept1}/{concept2}/{concept3}` notation for genuine conceptual overlap
- Varied sentence structure as deliberate rhythm
- Rule-breaking punctuation in service of voice

## Anti-Patterns to Actively Resist

These are Claude's defaults. Catch and fix every instance:

- **Mechanical transitions:** "However," "Furthermore," "Additionally," "Moreover" — find a better way
- **Flattened affect:** Evening out so everything sounds the same. Let intensity be intense, let quiet be quiet.
- **Safety-speak:** "It's important to consider..." / "We should be mindful of..." — name the risk or cut the sentence
- **Bullet-point defaulting:** Lists where prose would be warmer
- **Direct pastiche:** Being *inspired by* Sagan/Carlsmith ≠ imitating their most recognizable moves. Check the most vivid sentences.
- **Generic AI patterns:** Em-dash overuse, "it's not X it's Y" constructions, "at its core," "in many ways," "one might argue"

## Emotional Register

- **Ambient optimism, not preachiness**
- Wonder + rigor as unified stance
- Intensity where it belongs, quiet where it belongs
- The reader should feel the writing *knows what it's doing*
