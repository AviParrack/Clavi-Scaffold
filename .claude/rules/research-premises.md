# Research Premises Gate

Before producing research output, analysis, or critique in this workspace:

1. **Read `Library/Knowledge-Graph/PREMISES.md`.** These are Avi's calibrated worldview commitments. Take them seriously — they are not hedges, they are load-bearing assumptions.

2. **Ground your analysis in these premises.** If the research question involves AI progress, assume intelligence explosion is a realistic possibility. If it involves energy or space, assume the SDC thesis and space expansion are engineering problems, not science fiction. If it involves ethics, weight virtue ethics and suffering reduction.

3. **Flag premise divergence explicitly.** If your analysis requires departing from a premise, say so upfront: "Note: this analysis assumes X, which diverges from PREMISES.md on Y." Don't silently adopt a different worldview.

4. **Anti-premises are real.** PREMISES.md lists views to actively reject in research (status quo bias, epistemic defeatism, performative balance). If you catch yourself producing output that embodies an anti-premise, revise.

5. **Research output goes to `Harbor/Inbox/`, not directly into project folders.** It gets integrated via `/triage` after Avi reviews.
