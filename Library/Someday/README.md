# Someday

*Ideas that aren't yet projects. The 🟡 Yellow tier of `/triage`-able items.*

## What goes here

- Research questions you're curious about but not actively pursuing
- Project ideas that need more thought before greenlight
- Inspirations from books, papers, conversations
- Open threads — things you'd like to come back to "someday"

Each item should have just enough metadata that future-you can find it again:

```markdown
---
date: YYYY-MM-DD
tags: [topic-1, topic-2]
status: someday
trigger: "[what would make this active — a deadline, a new technique, a collaborator]"
---

# [Topic]

[1-2 paragraphs of what the idea is and why it caught your attention]

## Why someday and not now

[Honest about why this isn't being pursued. Capacity? Waiting on a capability? Need a collaborator?]
```

## Read patterns

- The `/memory-synthesis` skill periodically scans Someday and may surface items that newly match your active interests
- `/research-sprint` consults Someday before launching to avoid duplicating prior thinking
- Manually grep here when looking for "what was that idea I had about X"

This folder is empty in a fresh clone.
