# Ben Lay Day

*Based on Lizka Vaintrob's "Celebrating Benjamin Lay" (EA Forum, Feb 2024)*
*Part of the "Lyricized versions of writings from friends" album*

---

## Style prompt

`80s synth-pop, Tears for Fears, dreamy, atmospheric, clean guitar arpeggios, synth pads, reverb, male vocals, melancholic, driving, earnest, new wave`

## Lyrics

```
[Verse 1]
Four foot standing Ben Lay was odd
A Bible and a blade
He stood before those men of God
And cursed their wicked trade

He split the book, the red poured out
Like blood it pooled and stained
"Thus God shall judge the men," he cried
"Who keep their brothers chained"

[Chorus]

The only moral spine
For twenty-seven stubborn years
He came back each damn time

He wore no cotton from the bound
Tasted no stolen cane

Stepped over in the rain

He was shoeless, feet in the snow
No coat against the cold
"Your slaves endure far worse," he said
Time and again he told

He did not live to see it end

Against the tide and test
```

## Source

Benjamin Lay (1682-1759). Four-foot-tall Quaker abolitionist. Lived in a cave with 200 books. Spent 27 years disrupting Quaker meetings with guerrilla theater — stabbed a Bible full of pokeberry juice to splatter "blood" on slaveholders, stood barefoot in the snow outside meetings, kidnapped a slaveholder's child to show them how it feels. Was expelled from four meetings. Died at peace after the Quakers finally condemned slaveholding.

Lizka Vaintrob remembered him 265 years later.
