# Forethought Style as Default

All research output, writing, and publication work in this workspace should follow Forethought's stylistic and epistemic standards unless explicitly overridden. This applies to all skills that produce prose, research notes, or structured analysis — including third-party skills (sci-*, acad-*) and our custom skills (research-sprint, draft-it, forethought-post).

## When This Applies

- Any invocation of research-sprint, draft-it, forethought-post, forethought-publish
- Any invocation of acad-deep-research, acad-academic-paper, acad-academic-pipeline
- Any invocation of sci-scientific-writing, sci-literature-review, sci-peer-review
- Any freeform research, analysis, or writing task

## Style References (Read on First Use)

The authoritative Forethought style documents live in the forethought-starter submodule:

1. **Patterns & voice:** `Crossroads/forethought-starter/skills/forethought-style/references/forethought-patterns.md`
   - Institutional "we" with directional conviction
   - Lead with the take (first 200 words deliver core message)
   - Bolded topic sentences + 2-3 supporting sentences as primary prose unit
   - Visual variety required (prose, bullets, tables, callout boxes, diagrams)
   - Footnotes as parallel text for technical depth
   - Concrete-to-abstract movement

2. **Formatting:** `Crossroads/forethought-starter/skills/forethought-style/references/formatting.md`
   - Forethought orange: #E87040
   - Orange callout boxes for key findings; grey for context
   - Methodology/attribution box required
   - Section headers with ID anchors

3. **What makes a hit:** `Crossroads/forethought-starter/skills/forethought-post/references/retreat-insights.md`
   - Missile test: nugget-y payload + intellectual heft + real-world push
   - Cognitive-tool test: new way to think, not just new things to know
   - Barbell: ship fast or commit to hit; nothing in between

4. **Content types:** `Crossroads/forethought-starter/skills/forethought-style/references/style-guide.md`
   - Abstracts, tweet threads, Forum posts — each has specific patterns

5. **Examples:** `Crossroads/forethought-starter/skills/forethought-style/references/examples/`
   - 32 real Forethought pieces (abstracts, threads, posts)

## Core Anti-Patterns (Always Reject)

- Mechanical transitions ("However," "Furthermore," "Additionally")
- Safety-speak ("It's important to consider," "We should be mindful")
- Generic AI patterns (em-dash overuse, "at its core," "in many ways")
- Over-hedging ("One might perhaps suggest that X could potentially...")
- Performative balance where evidence points one way
- Prose walls (4+ paragraphs same format = something wrong)
- Burying the lead (most important finding must be in first 200 words)
- Flattened affect (let surprising findings surprise)

## Exceptions

- **draft-it** defaults to Avi's personal voice (Blog/02-style/README.md), not Forethought institutional voice. Forethought style still applies to epistemic standards and anti-patterns, but the register is first-person singular, not institutional "we."
- **When the user explicitly requests** a different style or register, follow their instruction.
- **Pure code/technical output** (scripts, configs, data processing) is exempt from prose style rules.
