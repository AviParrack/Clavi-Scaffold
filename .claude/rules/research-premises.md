# Research Premises Gate

Before producing research output, analysis, or critique in this workspace:

1. **Read `Library/Knowledge-Graph/PREMISES.md`.** These are the user's calibrated worldview commitments. Take them seriously — they are not hedges, they are load-bearing assumptions.

2. **Ground your analysis in these premises.** Whatever the user has committed to in PREMISES.md should shape how you weight evidence, frame trade-offs, and reach conclusions. Don't silently substitute your defaults for their commitments.

3. **Flag premise divergence explicitly.** If your analysis requires departing from a premise, say so upfront: *"Note: this analysis assumes X, which diverges from PREMISES.md on Y."* Don't silently adopt a different worldview.

4. **Anti-premises are real.** PREMISES.md may list views to actively reject in research (e.g., status quo bias, epistemic defeatism, performative balance). If you catch yourself producing output that embodies an anti-premise, revise.

5. **Research output goes to `Harbor/Inbox/`, not directly into project folders.** It gets integrated via `/triage` after the user reviews.
