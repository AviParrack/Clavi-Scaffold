## Mission

Help the user build a network that matches the ambition of their work. Read `Town-Hall/User/User.md` for who they are, what they're working on, and what kinds of connections matter.

## Strategy

### 1. Identify high-value connections

Categories worth tracking (replace with the user's actual fields):

- **Domain experts in the user's primary field** — researchers, practitioners, opinion leaders
- **Adjacent-field connectors** — people whose work intersects the user's in productive ways
- **Decision-makers / institutional access** — funders, editors, conference organizers, policy folks
- **Peers** — others at the user's career stage doing related work (mutual support + collaboration)
- **Mentors / mentor-figures** — people the user could learn from at the next level up
- **Community hubs** — people who connect lots of others; cultivating them multiplies reach

### 2. Find warm intro paths

For each prospective contact:
- Map who in the user's existing network knows them (read `Crossroads/Network.md`)
- Identify shared affiliations (alumni networks, mutual orgs, conference circuits)
- Look for online overlap (mutual followers, co-citations, collaborations)
- The strongest warm path is usually a peer or close collaborator who knows both sides

### 3. Lead with mutual benefit

The user has real things to offer (see `Town-Hall/User/User.md`). Lead with what they bring — research, analysis, platform, expertise — not with asks.

### 4. Calibrate over time

Every time the user rates a proposed connection (great fit / not interested / already know them), update `Harbor/Dispatch/scout-calibration.md` so future runs improve.

## Templates

### Outreach draft (cold)

When proposing a draft for the user to send (or for `/email-triage` to draft on the user's behalf), use the email playbook in [`playbook-email.md`](playbook-email.md). Key elements:

1. Disclosure of who's writing (if Claude drafts on the user's behalf)
2. Specific reason this person matters now — not generic "would love to connect"
3. A real ask with a real timeframe
4. The user's scheduling link (from `Town-Hall/User/Web-Presence/links.md`) when proposing meetings

### Tracking new contacts

When a contact is made or proposed:
- Add to `Crossroads/Network.md` (or `Network.md.example` template if not yet populated) with: relationship strength, how met, what they care about, last interaction, open threads
- Note any commitments ("said I'd send my draft by Friday") so the user can follow through

## How calibration accrues

- After each `/triage` of `/network-scout` output, log what was rated highly vs. skipped to `scout-calibration.md`
- Patterns worth tracking: "fits explicit gaps" / "warm path strength" / "intellectual fit" / "timing"
- Over weeks, the playbook gets specific to the user's actual network strategy
