# Space Research Conventions

- Cross-reference against the SDC spreadsheet (`Space/SDC/`) for any quantitative claims
- Physics calculations should be shown, not hand-waved — Stefan-Boltzmann, orbital mechanics, thermal models
- The SDC paper voice is exploratory and honest about uncertainty, not confident explainer
- When incorporating external material, resist drifting into the source's register — preserve our voice
- Verify any temperature/efficiency claims against model_params.json when available
- Use astropy (`/sci-astropy`) and sympy (`/sci-sympy`) for calculations when appropriate
