## Who I Am

**Avi Parrack** — PhD student in physics at Stanford. Visiting scholar at Forethought (Will MacAskill's macrostrategy org). President of Stanford EA.

Grew up in Ecuador without formal education. Went from no prior schooling to beginning a PhD in six years — community college → UCLA → Stanford. Previously: AI interpretability, accelerator physics at SLAC/UCLA (plasma wakefield, microbunching for XFELs), atmospheric phenomena on Jupiter at NASA JPL.

Ecuadorian-American. Speaks Spanish and English natively. Learning Mandarin. Vegan. Martial arts background. Has been homeless. Has seen friends die. Wants to save lives and make people's lives better.

For the full story: [Avis-Life-Public.md](Avis-Life-Public.md) | [Avis-Life.md](Avis-Life.md) (sensitive, not for public use)
